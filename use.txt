================================================================================
 CHAGent — Intended Use and Limitations
 ACSAC'26 Artifact (Paper #186)
================================================================================

INTENDED USE
------------
This artifact is a research prototype released to (a) allow independent
verification of the results reported in the paper and (b) support follow-up
research on translating natural-language access requirements into structured,
machine-enforceable access control policies.

It is intended to be run by artifact reviewers and researchers in a controlled
environment (see infrastructure/README.txt). Typical uses:
  - Reproducing the identification, generation, and verification results.
  - Studying the contribution of retrieval and iterative refinement (ablations).
  - Building on the models, prompts, and evaluation code for new research.

INTENDED SCOPE OF INPUTS
------------------------
  - English-language sentences describing access requirements (NLACPs).
  - The datasets shipped in artifact/data/ (t2p, acre, ibm, collected, cyber),
    which come from access-control corpora used in the paper.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
  - Research quality, not production: outputs are probabilistic and can be
    wrong (missing rules, incorrect subjects/resources, flipped decisions).
    The verifier reduces but does not eliminate such errors.
  - NOT a security enforcement mechanism. Generated policies MUST be reviewed
    by a human before being used to grant or deny real access. Do not wire the
    raw output into any live authorization system.
  - Language/domain bound: trained and evaluated on English text from specific
    domains; behavior on other languages or domains is untested.
  - Model dependence: the generator is a fine-tuned LLaMa-3-8B-Instruct and
    inherits that model's biases and failure modes; the base model is gated and
    subject to Meta's license terms.
  - Compute bound: a CUDA GPU is required. CPU-only execution is not supported
    for the LLaMa-3 generator or BART verifier at usable speed.
  - Determinism: generation uses greedy decoding, but exact metric values may
    vary across hardware, driver, and library versions.
