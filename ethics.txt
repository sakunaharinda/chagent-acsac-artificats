We have carefully considered the ethics in accordance with the conference guidelines. Our research aims to enhance the accuracy/performance of the automated access control policy generation process in a human-centric manner. In addition to the theoretical evaluation of our policy generation framework, we also implemented it as a system with a user interface and empirically evaluated it with professional policy implementers. To this end, we received approval from the Human Participants Ethics Committee of the lead author’s university for conducting the user study reported in the paper.
